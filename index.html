SARAK: A Geographic Information System for Business Infrastructure in Bangued, Abra

Sarak is a website with a map of important places in Bangued, Abra, like hospitals, pharmacies, coffee shops, fast-food chains, and groceries. Right now, the town's planning office only has a yearly spreadsheet of businesses, with no map locations or opening hours, and nobody to check what changes during the year. So people who ask for a place get directions from staff memory, which is often outdated, and they end up at places that are closed or moved. Sarak fixes this by letting residents and business owners add places, report changes, and upload photos. Planning office staff review every submission before it goes live, so the map stays accurate and trustworthy. Anyone can then use the map to search for places and get directions.
