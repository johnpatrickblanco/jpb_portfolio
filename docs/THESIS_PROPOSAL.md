## Proposal 1 — Casa Nostra Resort and Event Information System with Reservation System

### 1. Introduction
The Casa Nostra Resort and Event Information System with Reservation System is an online
platform that provides resort and event information while enabling a convenient online
reservation process. Visiting guests often rely on phone calls, walk-ins, and manual
records to inquire about availability and book amenities. These methods can be slow,
error-prone, and inconvenient, especially during peak seasons.

This project addresses those problems by providing a centralized information system
where guests can learn about the resort, check room availability, submit reservations,
and review their booking details — all in one place.

### 2. Problem Statement
The current reservation and information process is manual and fragmented, leading to:
- Difficulty for guests in checking real-time room and event availability
- Increased risk of double bookings and human error in record keeping
- Time-consuming back-and-forth communication between guests and staff
- Disorganized booking and event records that are hard to track and manage

### 3. Objectives
**General Objective:**
To design and develop an information system with an online reservation feature that
improves the booking experience for guests and organizes resort and event management.

**Specific Objectives:**
- To develop a system that provides complete resort and event information to guests
- To allow guests to check room availability and submit reservations online
- To enable guests to view their booking details
- To organize and manage booking records for resort personnel

### 4. Scope and Limitations
**Scope:**
- Resort and event information display
- Room availability checking
- Online reservation submission
- Booking details viewing

**Limitations:**
- Online payment processing is not included in this phase
- The system is designed for a single resort establishment
- Real-time notifications depend on connection availability

### 5. Methodology
The project follows a Systems Development Life Cycle (SDLC) approach, including planning,
analysis, design, implementation, and testing. Iterative feedback from the resort staff
guides each phase.

### 6. Technology Stack
- **Frontend:** HTML, CSS, JavaScript
- **Backend:** PHP
- **Database:** MySQL

### 7. Expected Outcome
An easy-to-use online system that simplifies reservation booking, reduces manual work,
and provides organized, accessible booking and event records.

## Proposal 2 — Bayugo Dental Clinic Online Record Management System

### 1. Introduction
The Bayugo Dental Clinic Online Record Management System is designed to help a dental
clinic organize patient information, appointments, and dental records. Dental clinics
frequently rely on paper-based records and manual appointment scheduling, which can be
difficult to search, update, and secure.

This project digitizes the clinic's record-keeping so that authorized personnel can
manage and access patient information more easily and reduce repetitive manual work.

### 2. Problem Statement
Manual record management creates several challenges for the clinic:
- Patient records are stored across paper files, making them slow to retrieve
- Appointment scheduling is prone to conflicts and double bookings
- Records are vulnerable to loss, damage, and unauthorized access
- Staff spend significant time on repetitive administrative tasks

### 3. Objectives
**General Objective:**
To develop an online record management system that organizes patient information,
appointments, and dental clinic records for easier management and access.

**Specific Objectives:**
- To store and organize patient information in a secure online system
- To manage appointments efficiently and reduce scheduling conflicts
- To provide authorized personnel with quick access to dental records
- To reduce manual work and improve accuracy in record keeping

### 4. Scope and Limitations
**Scope:**
- Patient information management
- Appointment management
- Dental clinic record storage and retrieval
- Role-based access for authorized personnel

**Limitations:**
- Medical billing and insurance integration are out of scope for this phase
- Access is limited to authorized clinic personnel
- Requires internet access to use the online system

### 5. Methodology
A structured SDLC approach is followed from planning and requirements analysis through
design, coding, testing, and deployment, with regular input from clinic staff.

### 6. Technology Stack
- **Frontend:** HTML, CSS, JavaScript
- **Backend:** PHP, Laravel
- **Database:** MySQL

### 7. Expected Outcome
A secure, organized online system that reduces manual work, makes patient information
easier to manage and access, and improves appointment handling for the clinic.
